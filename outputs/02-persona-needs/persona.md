# Persona & Needs — Marketing OS

> No direct user research exists yet (demo-sprint context). Everything below is `[hypothesis]` unless marked otherwise, and should be pressure-tested next.

## Lean Persona Card

1. **Name + Role:** Priya, Senior Campaign Manager at a ~3,000-person enterprise brand, reports to a Marketing Director, runs 3-4 concurrent campaigns `[hypothesis]`
2. **Primary Goal:** Get an approved campaign live across every channel on schedule, with a status she can trust and show her director — without babysitting five tools `[hypothesis]`
3. **Top 3 Frustrations:**
   - Logging into 4-5 separate platforms (ESP, social, ads, WhatsApp/SMS) to publish one campaign `[hypothesis]`
   - No trustworthy single status view — has to ping individual channel owners to know what's actually live `[hypothesis]`
   - A last-minute brand/legal change means redoing the same edit by hand in every tool `[hypothesis]`
4. **Context of Use:** Launch day and the days leading up to it; at her desk on a laptop; frequently interrupted by Slack pings from stakeholders asking "is it live yet?"; high time pressure around simultaneous launch windows (e.g., 9am email + social + web go live together) `[hypothesis]`
5. **One Quote:** "I don't need another tool to write copy — I need to stop being the one who manually checks five dashboards to know if my own campaign actually went out." `[representative]`

## Empathy Map

**Says**
- "Can someone confirm the email actually sent?" `[hypothesis]`
- "Why did the social post go out an hour late?" `[hypothesis]`

**Does**
- Keeps a manual spreadsheet/checklist cross-referencing what should launch where and when `[hypothesis]`
- Pings individual channel owners on Slack near launch time to confirm status `[hypothesis]`

**Thinks**
- "If this campaign launches inconsistently, it reflects on me, not on the channel owners." `[hypothesis]`
- "I don't fully trust that everything is scheduled correctly until I've checked it myself." `[hypothesis]`

**Feels**
- Anxious in the hours around a multi-channel launch — a failure is visible and hard to trace quickly `[hypothesis]`
- Relieved and confident once she has one reliable signal that everything actually fired `[hypothesis]`

**Contradiction worth noting:** She *says* "can someone confirm," implying she'd trust a report from others — but *thinks* she doesn't trust it until she checks herself. The real need isn't just a notification; it's a system she can independently verify without depending on people.

## User Goal Hierarchy

| Level | Statement |
|---|---|
| Immediate Desire | "I want to launch this campaign across all channels at the scheduled time and know it happened." `[hypothesis]` |
| Functional Motivation | "I need one place to schedule, launch, and verify status across channels so I'm not manually reconciling 5 tools every launch." `[hypothesis]` |
| Emotional/Social Expectation | "I want to feel in control of my own campaign's execution and be seen by my director as reliable and on top of things, not scrambling." `[hypothesis]` |

## Current Workaround
Priya maintains a manual checklist/spreadsheet mapping each channel to its own tool and scheduled time. She logs into each tool separately to schedule content, and on launch day pings channel owners on Slack (or checks each tool's dashboard herself) to confirm things actually went live — reconciling status by hand, often finding out about failures late or secondhand. `[hypothesis]`

## Prototype Usefulness Test
This prototype would feel genuinely useful to Priya if, after using it, she could schedule approved content once and watch a single live status view confirm every channel fired on time — **without pinging a single channel owner to check.**
